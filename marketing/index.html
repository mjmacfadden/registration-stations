Why Bottled Water Is the Ultimate Branding Test

Bottled water is one of the most undifferentiated products on earth — it's just water. Yet Liquid Death built a brand around "murdering your thirst" with punk-metal cans and reached a $1.4 billion valuation. Fiji Water charges roughly 3x the price of generic water with its "untouched by man" island narrative. Smartwater uses sleek minimalist branding and electrolyte promises to justify a premium price.

None of these companies changed the water itself. They changed the story and lifestyle customers connect with. That's your marketing challenge today.

Brand Name & Vibe Gemini Prompt

Act as a creative branding director. Suggest 5 bold, memorable brand names for a new bottled water company targeting [pick a niche: gamers / skaters / gym athletes / eco-warriors / busy high schoolers]. For each name, explain the vibe and why it stands out.

Tagline & Positioning Gemini Prompt
